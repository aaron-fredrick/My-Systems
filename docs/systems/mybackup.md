# 🗄️ MyBackup

## Own your recovery.

MyBackup is the planned central backup and recovery system for machines, services and My Systems.

## Scope

- Scheduled and on-demand backups
- Snapshots/version history
- Retention policies
- Encryption
- Restore workflows
- Restore verification
- Disaster recovery

## Design Principle

Backup is not the same as storage. MyDrive is active storage; MyBackup exists to recover from deletion, corruption, hardware failure or other loss.
