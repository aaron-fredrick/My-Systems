01 / MY SYSTEMS

Your systems.Your control.

An open-source family of self-hosted and self-managed systems for people who want greater privacy, ownership and control over their digital infrastructure.

SCROLL EXPLORE
02 / THE IDEA

The cloud is a tool.
Not a dependency.

My Systems is about practical ownership. Your infrastructure should work for you - not the other way around. Choose where your data lives, how your services operate, and how your systems connect. Use hosted services when they make sense, self-host when you want control, and keep the freedom to change either way.
