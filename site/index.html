05 / PRINCIPLES

Own.
Connect.
Operate.

01

Own what matters.

Self-host where control, privacy, resilience or learning provides a meaningful advantage.

02

Independent first.

Every system should remain useful on its own. Integration is an option, not a trap.

03

Open by default.

Prefer open standards, portable deployments and interfaces that do not lock the ecosystem in.

04

Security is infrastructure.

Identity, secrets, recovery and operational visibility are designed into the system family.
